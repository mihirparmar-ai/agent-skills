The Itinerary Builder

One agent. It drafts a trip from what the house knows about a client, checks its own draft against the house rules and the client's profile, and hands the advisor a proposal with the reasoning and the questions it could not settle. Synthetic data throughout.

What an AI agent is

A chat

A model answers a question. The human is the loop, the memory, the tool-caller, and the verifier. Ask a chatbot for a Japan itinerary and you get a plausible one; you still check the hours, you still remember what the client said last spring, and you still decide.

An agent

A model inside a loop with tools, state, and a stopping policy. It observes, decides the next action, acts through a tool, checks the result, and continues, finishes, or escalates. The product work is the stopping policy: where it acts alone, where it drafts, where it hands off.

What the house knows, as data

Explicit

Intake form and onboarding call.

No beef. One theme park at most. Must-haves: teamLab, a ninja experience, one kaiseki dinner.

becomes a venue-tag exclusion, a per-trip cap, three must-have checks, an age floor

Conversational

Dated quotes from calls and messages.

"We are not a 6 a.m. family. Nine is early for us on holiday." Parent B, May 20

becomes a 09:30 floor on every start; a two-temple daily cap from another quote

Behavioral

Past trips: booked, raved about, skipped, the lesson.

Italy 2025: left the second museum after 40 minutes. Costa Rica 2024: cancelled the 05:30 walk.

becomes one anchor a morning, one hands-on thing an afternoon, nothing before nine

compile prints every constraint with the signal it came from. That output is the personalization receipt: the signal, the rule it became, and where it applies.

The loop, on the synthetic brief

call get_brief          Jul 13 to 17, dates fixed (school holidays)
call get_profile        13 constraints, floor 09:30, youngest 8
call lookup_destination Tokyo: Jul is an avoid month
call list_venues        11 allowed, 3 dropped (dawn, beef, adults)
call draft_itinerary    draft 1
     11 fixable: closure, ticketing, buffer, jetlag,
                 group_fit, contingency x2, profile x4
call draft_itinerary    draft 2
     0 fixable; timing x2 go to the advisor
call finalize           rationale per day, 2 questions

The shape of a run. Draft 1 above is the golden faulty case resubmitted; draft 2 is the advisor-approved reference. Both were run through the tool layer without a model; a live model run is not yet verified on this machine.

What it escalates, on purpose

July is an avoid month and the dates are fixed. The kaiseki seats no one under ten and the youngest is eight.

Neither is a bug in the rules. The agent mitigates (indoor afternoons), may propose the dinner as adults-only with a sitter, and raises both with the evidence. The advisor's answer is expressible in the data, which is how the reference draft passes with only the month left open.

How it is measured

Golden set

100% · 100%

Recall and precision of the checker over three cases and 23 expected findings: every rule planted once; profile faults only; an advisor-approved reference where only the month remains.

Building it found one checker bug and two gaps in the human expectations.

Scorecard

6 metrics

Hard constraints, must-have coverage, profile violations, escalation coverage, rationale traceability, overlap with the reference. Runs on any draft, including the agent's next one.

The honest edges

  • Rules where a fact is cheaply verifiable, a model where judgment is needed, a person where the trip is at stake. The checker never misses a rule it has and never finds a problem it has no rule for; the model composes and can be wrong; the advisor decides.
  • Not wired to the website. The Second Opinion form is the eventual entry point.
  • Not live rules. Hours and ticket windows are cached with a source and a checked-on date.
  • Not client data. The family, the quotes, and the past trips are invented.
  • Not a booking agent. The write list has one entry and it is a findings list.

The bridge line

Where does it act alone, where does it draft, where does it escalate.

A servicing agent mapped to specific APIs, running a deterministic workflow, that cannot invent a refund amount, is the same shape: tools to verify, a loop across days, state across trips, and a hard boundary on what it can touch.
